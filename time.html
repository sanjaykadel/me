About Nepal time

Nepal Standard Time (NPT) is UTC+5:45, one of the few time zones in the world with a 45-minute offset. Kathmandu, Pokhara and the rest of the country use the same time, and Nepal does not change its clocks for daylight saving time.

Because Nepal stays fixed while other countries move their clocks, the gap between Nepal and places like the USA, UK or Australia changes twice a year. The comparison above recalculates it from your browser's time zone, so it stays right during those changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time zone of Nepal?

Nepal uses Nepal Time (NPT), which is UTC+5:45 all year. The IANA time zone name is Asia/Kathmandu.

Does Nepal use daylight saving time?

No. Nepal stays on UTC+5:45 all year. The difference with countries that change their clocks, such as the USA, UK and Australia, changes by one hour when they switch.

How many hours ahead is Nepal?

It depends on where you are. Nepal is ahead of any place with a UTC offset below +5:45, such as the UK, most of Europe and the Americas, and behind places with a higher offset, such as Japan or eastern Australia. The calculator on this page works it out from your browser's time zone.

What is the time difference between Nepal and India?

India uses UTC+5:30 all year, so Nepal is always 15 minutes ahead of India.

Why is Nepal's offset 45 minutes?

Nepal chose UTC+5:45 in 1986 so that its standard time is close to the local solar time of Mount Everest and Kathmandu, and to set it apart from India's UTC+5:30.

How can I calculate the time difference between Nepal and Japan, South Korea, the Gulf or Malaysia?

These countries do not use daylight saving time, so the gap is constant. Nepal is 3 hours 15 minutes behind Japan and South Korea, 2 hours 15 minutes behind Malaysia, 1 hour 45 minutes ahead of the UAE, and 2 hours 45 minutes ahead of Qatar, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.
