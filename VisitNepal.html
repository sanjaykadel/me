Best Time to Visit Nepal: Complete Guide for Trekking, Festivals & Wildlife

Wondering when to book your trip? This season-by-season guide breaks down Nepal's weather, trekking conditions, festivals, wildlife safaris and budget seasons — so you can pick the perfect month for your adventure.

✍️ By Sanjay Kadel ⏱️ 12 min read 🏔️ Trekking · Festivals · Safari

Quick Answer

October to November is the best time to visit Nepal for most travellers — expect clear mountain views, comfortable temperatures, dry trekking trails and vibrant festivals.

March to April is the second-best window, ideal for trekking, sightseeing and seeing Nepal's famous rhododendron forests in full bloom.

Visiting Nepal in Autumn: The Best Season

Autumn

Late September – November · Peak Season

Autumn is widely considered the best season to visit Nepal. The monsoon rains usually disappear by late September, and as the skies clear you can enjoy spectacular views of Mount Everest, Annapurna, Langtang and other Himalayan peaks. Temperatures are comfortable in Kathmandu, Pokhara and popular trekking regions.

Why visit Nepal in autumn?

  • Clear, unobstructed mountain views
  • Dry and comfortable trekking trails
  • Pleasant temperatures for sightseeing
  • Excellent conditions for hiking and photography
  • Major festivals such as Dashain and Tihar
  • A lively atmosphere in tourist destinations
Plan ahead Autumn is Nepal's busiest tourist season. Flights, hotels, guides and trekking permits can be more expensive — booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Visiting Nepal in Spring: The Second-Best Season

Spring

March – May · Shoulder Season

Spring is the second-best season for most visitors. Days become warmer, the hills turn colourful and rhododendrons bloom across many trekking areas. It's especially popular in the Everest, Annapurna, Langtang and Manaslu regions.

Why visit Nepal in spring?

  • Warm and pleasant daytime weather
  • Beautiful rhododendron forests
  • Good trekking conditions
  • Suitable for cultural tours and sightseeing
  • Fewer visitors than during the autumn peak
  • Excellent opportunities for nature photography
Insider tip March and April are usually better than May for mountain views. By late spring, temperatures rise and haze becomes more noticeable in lower-altitude areas.

Visiting Nepal in Winter: Quiet Season

Winter

December – February · Low Season

Winter can be cold in the mountains, but lower-altitude destinations such as Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and Lumbini remain enjoyable. Skies are often clear and tourist numbers are far lower than in autumn and spring.

Why visit Nepal in winter?

  • Fewer tourists
  • Lower prices for hotels and tours
  • Clear mornings in many areas
  • Good conditions for city sightseeing
  • Possibility of snowfall in high-altitude regions
  • Excellent time for a Chitwan jungle safari
Trekking warning High-altitude treks can be difficult in winter due to freezing temperatures, snow and closed mountain passes. Choose a lower-altitude route and pack suitable warm clothing.

Visiting Nepal During the Monsoon: Budget Season

Monsoon

June – September · Off Season

The monsoon brings frequent rain, cloudy skies and humid conditions to much of Nepal. Roads and trekking trails may become muddy, and landslides can sometimes affect transportation in hilly areas.

However, monsoon is not a complete travel ban. It can be a rewarding season for travellers who enjoy green landscapes, quiet trails and lower prices.

Why visit Nepal during monsoon?

  • Lush green hills and forests
  • Fewer tourists
  • Lower accommodation prices
  • Good opportunities for cultural tours
  • Suitable conditions in rain-shadow areas such as Upper Mustang and parts of Dolpo
  • A peaceful experience away from crowded trails
Monsoon travel tips Carry waterproof clothing, allow extra travel time and check road and flight conditions before departing.

Best Time for Trekking in Nepal

The best trekking seasons in Nepal are generally October to November and March to April.

  • Autumn: stable weather, dry trails and excellent visibility
  • Spring: warmer conditions and colourful flowers, though mountain views may be slightly hazier

Popular trekking routes in Nepal

  • Everest Base Camp Trek
  • Annapurna Circuit Trek
  • Annapurna Base Camp Trek
  • Langtang Valley Trek
  • Manaslu Circuit Trek
  • Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek
  • Upper Mustang Trek
Which route suits you? Beginners or travellers with limited time may prefer the Ghorepani Poon Hill or Langtang Valley routes. More experienced trekkers can consider high-altitude routes such as Everest Base Camp or the Annapurna Circuit.

Best Time to Visit Kathmandu and Pokhara

The best time to visit Kathmandu and Pokhara is usually October to November and March to April.

Kathmandu

Kathmandu is ideal for exploring temples, palaces, museums and historic neighbourhoods. Popular attractions include Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath Temple and Boudhanath Stupa.

Pokhara

Pokhara is known for Phewa Lake, mountain views, caves, waterfalls, paragliding and access to the Annapurna region. October and November are particularly popular because the skies are often clear and the Annapurna range can be seen from the city.

Best Time for Wildlife Safari in Nepal

The best time for a wildlife safari in Nepal is generally October to March.

Chitwan National Park and Bardia National Park are popular destinations for spotting rhinoceroses, crocodiles, deer, wild elephants and many bird species. The weather is more comfortable during the cooler months, especially for early-morning jungle activities.

  • October to December: best balance of comfortable temperatures and wildlife activity
  • January and February: can be foggy in the lowlands
  • March: becomes warmer

Best Time for Nepal Festivals

Nepal has festivals throughout the year, but autumn is particularly important.

Dashain (September–October)

Dashain is Nepal's largest Hindu festival. Families gather, receive blessings and celebrate with traditional food and rituals. Because many people travel to their home villages during Dashain, some businesses and transport services may operate on reduced schedules.

Tihar (October–November)

Tihar, also known as the festival of lights, usually follows Dashain. Homes, streets and temples are decorated with oil lamps, candles and colourful designs. The atmosphere is especially beautiful in Kathmandu and other cities.

Holi (March)

Holi, the festival of colours, is generally celebrated in March. Visitors can experience music, dancing and colourful powder celebrations in cities and towns across Nepal.

Check the dates Festival dates change each year according to the lunar calendar, so verify them before planning your trip.

Cheapest Time to Visit Nepal

The monsoon months of June to August are often among the least expensive times to visit Nepal because tourist demand is lower. January and February can also offer better prices, particularly in cities and lower-altitude areas.

What you can save on

  • Hotels and guesthouses
  • Domestic tours
  • Trekking packages
  • Flights and transportation
  • Guides and porters
Trade-offs Low prices may come with rain, reduced mountain visibility, flight delays and difficult road conditions.

Travel Tips: Choosing the Right Season

Choose your travel season according to your main activity:

  • October or November — clearest mountain views and classic trekking
  • March or April — pleasant weather and rhododendron flowers
  • December to February — quieter cities, wildlife safaris and possible snowfall
  • June to August — lower prices and green landscapes
  • September to November — festivals and cultural experiences
  • Monsoon — choose a rain-shadow destination if trekking is your priority

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month to visit Nepal?

October and November are generally the best months for clear skies, trekking and mountain views.

Is Nepal good to visit during the monsoon?

Yes. Monsoon is suitable for cultural tours, green landscapes, budget travel and rain-shadow areas such as Upper Mustang, although rain can affect roads and mountain visibility.

What is the best time for Everest Base Camp?

October to November and March to April are generally the most suitable periods for the Everest Base Camp trek.

When is Nepal least crowded?

The monsoon months from June to August and the colder winter months are usually less crowded.

How many days are enough for Nepal?

A 7–10 day trip is suitable for Kathmandu, Pokhara and a short trek or safari. A 14–21 day itinerary allows time for a longer trek and several regions.

Conclusion: When Should You Visit Nepal?

The best time to visit Nepal is October to November for most travellers, thanks to clear skies, comfortable temperatures, excellent mountain visibility and favourable trekking conditions. Spring, especially March and April, is another excellent choice for nature lovers and trekkers.

Nevertheless, Nepal can be visited throughout the year. Your ideal travel season depends on whether you prefer trekking, festivals, wildlife, snowfall, greenery, fewer crowds or lower prices.

Ready to Plan Your Nepal Adventure?

Pick your season, book early for peak months, and pack for the altitude. Nepal rewards travellers in every month of the year.
